Remote promotion and ticketing partner

Convert an existing audience without paid advertising while responding to a location change shortly before the event. Buyers had to be redirected.

Case study 03

LIL XAN AT EMPIRE ATX

Coordinating a concert across multiple ticket channels

Overview

Venue
Empire ATX
Collaboration
Co-presented with 512 Events and collaborators
Scope
Multiple ticket channels, promotion, coordination and financial tracking

This event was not produced alone.

My role

Co-presenter responsible for D42PE ticketing, promotion, coordination and financial tracking

Challenge

Coordinate a concert with 512 Events and collaborators across Universe and Ticketmaster while keeping the door estimate, verified ticketing results and planned budget distinct.

What I executed

  • D42PE ticketing
  • Promotion
  • Coordination
  • Financial tracking

Important decision or obstacle

Even though the artist was famous, with millions of followers and listeners, our crowd didn't fit his vibe well; therefore, conversion from our audience was lower than normal.

Lessons

  • Verified platform results, my door estimate and the planned budget are reported separately.

  • No final profit or loss is published, and unresolved merchandise information is excluded.
